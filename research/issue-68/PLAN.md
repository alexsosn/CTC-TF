## Architecture decision (prototype, not released)

A true occurrence requires one TF `entity` node per unambiguously aligned Burns occurrence, with source headword, root, source-defined thematic category, worksheet role, semantic status and section as **scalar TF features**. Its `oslots` covers the exact CUC word-span sign slots. Original CUC nodes retain IDs and sign-slot coordinates, but `otype`/`oslots` must be replaced by a coherent extended warp, not presented as an ordinary feature-only CUC overlay. Status `homograph_excluded` is queryable on its own entity: **a category-only query means Burns workbook membership, not a positive assertion; filter `burns_semantic_status` for positive-only research**. No `burns_lemma` is fabricated. Structural-only/ambiguous/out-of-CUC/nontextual occurrences stay accounted for in the sidecar without invented word hits.

## Finalization state after coverage gates

1. **Reference coverage is bounded deliberately.** Real CI classifies 172 residual parse failures (85 invalid KTU, 49 uncertainty-marked, 19 malformed, 10 duplicate-target, 8 wording, 1 punctuation) plus 21 parsed-but-absent CUC lines. No remaining class has a source-supported exact normalization that preserves uncertainty and authored anomalies, so no coercive parser rule is added.
2. **Lexical coverage is bounded deliberately.** Real CI materializes 4,357 exact lexical entities and keeps 4,993 resolved-line occurrences structural-only. The permanent aggregate gap audit shows the latter are overwhelmingly multi-token/partial-or-zero surface overlap; CUC 0.2.8 has no lemma/morphology layer. Prefix/suffix/fuzzy projection is therefore outside the exact native contract.
